ℹ️ What is rnaflow_server.py? — and how to start it on your OS Works on macOS · Linux · Windows · WSL2 Needed for Mode A only ▶
What is it?
A tiny Python script — no extra packages needed — that runs only on your own computer. It creates a bridge between this HTML app and your terminal so the app can send commands and receive live output.

It listens on 127.0.0.1:7788 — your local machine only. No other computer on the internet or your network can see or reach it.

Each person who uses RNAflow runs their own private copy locally. It is architecturally impossible for one person's server to interfere with another's.
🔒 100% local. Runs on your computer. Stops when you press Ctrl+C. Your data never leaves your machine.

R
# Standard: significant genes (padj < 0.05)
sig <- res_df[res_df$padj < 0.05 & !is.na(res_df$padj), ]

# Strict: significant AND at least 2-fold change
sig_strict <- res_df[res_df$padj < 0.05 & abs(res_df$log2FoldChange) > 1 & !is.na(res_df$padj), ]

# Top 20 most significant
top20 <- head(sig[order(sig$padj), ], 20)
print(top20[, c("gene", "log2FoldChange", "padj")])
