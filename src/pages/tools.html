RNAflow

Available

A guided desktop app for bulk RNA-seq analysis, from raw FASTQ files to differential expression and pathway enrichment. It writes the commands for your project, runs them with one click, and explains each result. Includes a small RNA route and can start from a count table.

Roadmap

These are not available yet and have no release dates. The order below is roughly the order of work.

GeneNet LabIn development

Self-hosted lab notebook and sample inventory with an audit trail, electronic signatures and automatic backups.

Read the details
NGSuiteIn development

Whole-genome and exome sequencing: variant calling with GATK, SNP and indel annotation, structural variants.

PlasmidMapIn development

Plasmid annotation: import GenBank files, find restriction sites, and export circular maps for publication.

ChIPflowPlanned

ChIP-seq and ATAC-seq: peak calling with MACS2, motif analysis with HOMER, differential accessibility.

MetaFlowPlanned

Metagenomics and 16S rRNA: taxonomic classification, alpha and beta diversity, differential abundance.

scRNAflowPlanned

Single-cell RNA-seq with Seurat: clustering, UMAP, marker genes and trajectory analysis.

ProteomicsLabPlanned

Mass spectrometry proteomics: label-free and TMT quantification, PTM analysis, pathway enrichment.
