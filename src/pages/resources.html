🔗 Curated Links

Bioinformatics Resources

A hand-picked directory of the most useful bioinformatics websites, databases, and tools — so you never lose track of them. Every link opens in a new tab.

🧬 Sequences & Genomes
NCBI ↗
ncbi.nlm.nih.gov
The central hub of 40+ databases — genes, genomes, proteins & literature.
BLAST ↗
blast.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov
Find regions of similarity between your sequence and known ones.

ensembl.org
Genome browser & annotation across vertebrates and other species.
UCSC Genome Browser ↗
genome.ucsc.edu
Explore genomes visually with rich, layered data tracks.
EMBL-EBI ↗
ebi.ac.uk
Europe's collection of molecular biology databases and tools.

Comprehensive protein sequences, function & annotation.

Experimentally determined 3D structures of biomolecules.

🌐 Run in Your Browser
Biomni ↗
biomni.phylo.bio
A biomedical AI agent from the Stanford SNAP lab. Ask a research question in plain English; it picks the datasets and tools, writes the code and interprets the results. 150 tools and 59 databases behind one prompt box.
Protenix Server ↗
protenix-server.com
AlphaFold3-class prediction for proteins, nucleic acids and ligands. ByteDance's open reproduction of AF3, Apache-licensed, with binder design through PXDesign on the same server.
AlphaFold Server ↗
alphafoldserver.com
DeepMind's own AlphaFold 3 for complexes of protein, DNA, RNA, ligands and ions. Free for non-commercial use.
Neurosnap ↗
neurosnap.ai
One account, many models — Boltz-2, Chai-1, Protenix, DiffDock, RoseTTAFold — all run server-side with nothing to install.
Foldseek Search ↗
search.foldseek.com
Search a structure against 200M+ predicted structures in seconds. Orders of magnitude faster than sequence-based search, and it finds homologues sequence alignment misses.
Galaxy ↗
usegalaxy.org
A full bioinformatics workbench in a browser: RNA-seq, variant calling, ChIP-seq. Free compute, and every analysis is reproducible as a shareable workflow.
EBI Job Dispatcher ↗
ebi.ac.uk/jdispatcher
BLAST, Clustal Omega, HMMER, InterProScan and 30 more, submitted from a form and emailed back. The dependable way to run one-off jobs.
SWISS-MODEL ↗
swissmodel.expasy.org
Homology modelling that has been free and browser-based since long before AI folding. Still the quickest route to a model when a good template exists.
MPI Bioinformatics Toolkit ↗
toolkit.tuebingen.mpg.de
HHpred and friends for remote-homology detection — the tool of choice when BLAST returns nothing and you need deeper sensitivity.
