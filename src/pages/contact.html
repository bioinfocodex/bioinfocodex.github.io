Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you send a message.

Is RNAflow really free? ▼
Yes. RNAflow is open source under the MIT licence, so you can use, copy and modify it, including for commercial work. There is no subscription, no licence key and no registration. The course on this site is free to read.
Does my data get uploaded anywhere? ▼
No. Analyses run on your own computer and your reads are never uploaded. The app and its small local server talk to each other over localhost only. The only requests that leave your machine are public lookups, such as fetching a GEO accession or a genome from Ensembl.
I found a bug. How do I report it? ▼
Open an issue on GitHub and say what you did, what you expected and what happened, with any error message. The contact form works too if you do not have a GitHub account.
Can I contribute? ▼
Yes. Pull requests are welcome; the repository's CONTRIBUTING file explains how the app is built and tested. Corrections to the course are just as useful: tell us the page and what is wrong.
How do I cite RNAflow? ▼
Through its Zenodo DOI. The How to cite page has the reference, a BibTeX entry and the tools to cite alongside it.
